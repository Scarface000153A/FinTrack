Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

FinTrack collects only the necessary information required to operate your personal finance account:

  • Account Information: Full name, username, email address, and hashed password.
  • Financial Records: Transaction amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and currency codes entered directly by you.
  • Preferences: Preferred display currency and interface theme mode (stored locally on your device).

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use your information exclusively to provide and improve your experience:

  • To authenticate your login sessions and maintain account security.
  • To calculate net balances, savings rates, and category summaries.
  • To convert transaction amounts dynamically into your selected display currency.
  • To generate downloadable CSV and JSON export files upon your explicit request.

3. Data Protection and Sandboxing

Your financial records are strictly isolated. Database queries use relational user identifiers to ensure no user can access or view another user's financial details. Passwords are encrypted using irreversible cryptographic hashing algorithms.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or financial logs with any third parties or advertising networks. Live currency exchange rate calculations query public market rates without transmitting any user identifying data.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

FinTrack uses secure HTTP session cookies solely to maintain your active login state. Your theme preference (dark or light mode) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is not transmitted to external servers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your transaction data remains in your personal database records until you delete individual records or request account removal. You can export complete copies of your data at any time via the dashboard.

7. Policy Updates

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
